How it works
Live badges that prove your security

Unlike screenshots or self-reported scores, PenChecker badges are verified in real time and link back to your public scan record.

01
Scan your website
Run a PenChecker scan on your domain. When it completes, your security score is stored in our database against your verified domain.
02
Customise your badge
Pick a style above — Dark, Light, Compact, or Detailed. The builder shows a live preview. Copy the embed code with one click.
03
Paste and it stays live
Add the code to your site. Your badge score updates automatically after every scan — no need to update the embed code ever again.
Verification
Every badge is verifiable by your visitors

When a visitor clicks your badge, they are taken to a public verification page showing your domain, scan date, score, and a breakdown of what was tested. No way to fake it.

  • Cryptographically signed — each badge carries a unique token tied to your verified domain
  • Publicly accessible — the verification page is available to anyone with the link
  • Score history shown — visitors can see your last 5 scan scores, not just the latest
  • Auto-expires if unscanned — badge dims after 90 days without a new scan, keeping scores honest
