Our mission

Make accurate security
accessible to everyone.

"Security tools should tell you the truth about your website — not scare you with invented vulnerabilities or drown you in jargon nobody understands."

Most security scanners were built for enterprise companies with dedicated security teams. They produce reports full of technical language, false positives, and templated findings that don't reflect reality.

We built PenChecker for the developer launching a startup at 2am, the small business owner who just wants to know if their site is safe, and the growing team that can't afford a $500/month tool built for Fortune 500 companies.

Our scanner verifies every finding before it reaches your report. Our AI explains what it means and writes the fix code for your exact tech stack. And our pricing is built to work for teams everywhere that deserve honest, accurate security tools without enterprise-level pricing.

The problem we solve

Why existing tools
kept failing us

Before building PenChecker, we documented exactly what was wrong with every other scanner on the market.

Problem 01
Template-based scanning
Scanners run the same checklist against every site and output the same findings regardless of what technology the site actually uses. We saw SQL injection reported on pure static HTML sites. The results are fiction.
Problem 02
Unverified findings with no evidence
Most tools report a vulnerability and give you nothing but a label. No server response. No proof it actually triggered. No confidence score. Just "SQL Injection found" — with no way to verify if it's real.
Problem 03
Reports nobody outside security can understand
CVSS vectors, CWE numbers, and raw HTTP dumps. Useful for specialists. Useless for the startup founder who just wants to know if their checkout page is safe. Security reports shouldn't require a degree to read.
Problem 04
Pricing built for enterprise, not the world
The leading security tools cost $200–$600 per month in USD only. Developers and startups worldwide are priced out entirely. Serious security should not require an enterprise budget.
Our answer
PenChecker — built to fix all four
Technology-aware scanning. Double-verified findings with evidence. Plain English toggle for every report. AI that writes the fix code. And local currency pricing from day one. This is what a modern security scanner should be.

Legal & ethical commitment

We protect you,
and protect others from you.

Security scanning is a powerful tool. In the wrong hands or used without permission, it becomes an attack. We take this responsibility seriously and have built multiple layers of protection into the platform.

Consent logged with timestamp Every scan requires domain ownership confirmation. User ID, target URL, IP address, and timestamp are permanently stored.
Domain blacklist enforced High-profile domains (Google, Facebook, government sites) are permanently blocked from being scanned on our platform.
Domain ownership verification Users must verify domain ownership via DNS TXT record or file upload before active scans are allowed.
Abuse detection & IP blocking Suspicious scan patterns trigger automatic flagging. Repeated abuse results in account and IP suspension.
