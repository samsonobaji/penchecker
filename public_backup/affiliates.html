Frequently asked questions
Who can join the affiliate program? ›
Anyone with an audience of developers, security professionals, or business owners. Bloggers, YouTubers, newsletter writers, Twitter/X creators, podcasters — if you can reach our target audience, we want you.
When and how do I get paid? ›
Payouts happen on the 1st of every month for the previous month's earnings. We pay via PayPal, bank transfer, or Wise. Minimum payout threshold is $50. Earnings below $50 roll over to the next month.
What happens if a customer upgrades their plan? ›
You earn commission on the upgraded amount too. If someone signs up for Individual and then upgrades to Team, your commission increases proportionally from the month they upgrade.
Can I refer my own account? ›
No — self-referrals are not permitted and will result in commission reversal and potential account suspension. Our system automatically detects self-referrals.
Is there a minimum traffic requirement to join? ›
No. We evaluate applications holistically — a small niche newsletter with 500 targeted subscribers can be more valuable than a general blog with 50,000. Quality of audience matters more than size.
