Acceptable Use Policy

Effective April 1, 2025
Version 1.0
PenChecker Inc.
Critical Legal Notice

Unauthorized scanning of systems you do not own or lack permission to test is illegal. The Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA) and equivalent laws in 190+ countries criminalize unauthorized access to computer systems. PenChecker cooperates fully with law enforcement investigations. Violations will result in immediate account termination and may result in criminal referral.

Plain English Summary

You may only use PenChecker on websites and systems you own or have explicit written permission to test. Unauthorized scanning is illegal. Certain high-profile domains (Google, government sites, etc.) are permanently blocked. Violations result in immediate account termination and may be reported to law enforcement. If you find a vulnerability in PenChecker itself, please report it responsibly to security@penchecker.io.

Section 01

Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules for lawful, ethical use of the PenChecker platform. Security testing tools are powerful — and with that power comes legal and ethical responsibility. This policy exists to protect you, third parties, and the integrity of the platform.

By using PenChecker, you agree to this AUP in addition to our Terms of Service. This AUP is incorporated by reference into the Terms.

Section 02

Who May Use PenChecker

PenChecker may be used by:

  • Website and web application owners testing their own systems
  • Security professionals with written authorization from system owners
  • Developers and DevOps teams testing systems they are responsible for
  • Organizations conducting authorized internal security assessments
  • Penetration testers operating under a signed engagement agreement

You may not use PenChecker if you are:

  • Under 18 years of age
  • Located in a jurisdiction where such tools are prohibited by law
  • Previously banned from the platform
  • Attempting to test systems without authorization

Section 04

Prohibited Activities (Full List)

The following activities are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate account termination:

4.1 Unauthorized Scanning

Scanning any domain, IP address, system, API, or application without clear ownership or written authorization from the owner. This includes but is not limited to: scanning competitor sites, scanning random domains to look for vulnerabilities, or conducting reconnaissance without authorization.

4.2 Exploitation

Using information found in PenChecker scan reports to actively exploit, attack, or compromise systems. PenChecker is a detection and remediation tool only — not an exploitation platform.

4.3 System Abuse

  • Creating multiple accounts to circumvent Free plan limits or evade bans
  • Using automated scripts to submit excessive scans in violation of plan limits
  • Attempting to access other users' scan data or reports
  • Injecting malicious content into scan inputs to attack PenChecker infrastructure

4.4 Illegal Activities

  • Using the platform in violation of any applicable federal, state, or international law
  • Scanning systems in connection with criminal activity, fraud, or harassment
  • Using the platform to facilitate unauthorized access to computer systems (CFAA violation)

4.5 Intellectual Property Violations

  • Attempting to copy, clone, or replicate PenChecker's scanning technology
  • Using PenChecker's brand, name, or reports in misleading commercial contexts
Section 05

Domain Ownership & Consent Requirements

Before running any active scan, you must confirm that you own the target domain or have written permission to test it. This confirmation is:

  • Logged permanently — your user ID, target URL, IP address, and timestamp
  • Legally binding — by confirming, you make a representation of fact
  • Non-deletable — these records are retained indefinitely for legal compliance

For domains where ownership verification is required, you may verify via:

  • DNS TXT record verification
  • File upload verification (placing a verification file on the domain)
  • Meta tag verification
Penetration Testers

If you are a professional penetration tester scanning client systems, we recommend uploading a copy of your signed engagement agreement to your account documentation. This protects both you and your client in the event of any legal question about authorization.

Section 06

Blacklisted Domains

The following categories of domains are permanently blocked from scanning on PenChecker, regardless of claimed ownership or permission:

  • Major consumer platforms — Google, Facebook, YouTube, Twitter/X, Instagram, Amazon, Microsoft, Apple, Netflix, LinkedIn, TikTok, WhatsApp, and similar high-profile targets
  • US Government domains — .gov and .mil domains including but not limited to: whitehouse.gov, cia.gov, fbi.gov, nsa.gov, dhs.gov, pentagon.mil, irs.gov, and all US federal agencies
  • International government and military domains — Government and military domains of all nations
  • International organizations — UN, WHO, IMF, World Bank, and similar organizations
  • Financial infrastructure — Federal Reserve, SWIFT, major stock exchanges, and core financial infrastructure

Attempting to scan a blacklisted domain will be flagged, logged, and may result in immediate account suspension. Repeated attempts will result in permanent termination and may be referred to relevant authorities.

Blacklist Management

The blacklist is maintained and updated by PenChecker administrators. To request removal of a legitimate domain from the blacklist (e.g., a company that has acquired one of the listed domains), contact security@penchecker.io with proof of ownership.

Section 07

Rate Limits & Abuse Detection

Each plan has defined scan limits (see Pricing). Attempting to circumvent these limits through automation, multiple accounts, or technical exploits is a violation of this policy.

Our abuse detection system monitors for:

  • Unusually high scan volumes from a single IP or account
  • Attempts to scan the same target repeatedly in a short period
  • Patterns consistent with reconnaissance or coordinated attack activity
  • Multiple failed attempts to scan blacklisted domains

Flagged accounts may be temporarily suspended pending review. Our security team reviews all flags within 24 hours.

Section 08

Consequences of Violations

Violations of this AUP may result in, at our sole discretion:

  1. Warning — For minor first-time violations
  2. Temporary suspension — Account access suspended pending investigation (1–30 days)
  3. Immediate termination — Account deleted without refund for serious violations
  4. IP ban — Your IP address blocked from the platform
  5. Legal referral — Reporting to relevant law enforcement agencies for criminal violations
  6. Civil action — PenChecker Inc. reserves the right to pursue civil damages for violations that cause harm to the platform or third parties

We take the safety of third-party systems seriously. If we have evidence that a user has used PenChecker to facilitate an unauthorized attack, we will cooperate with law enforcement and the affected parties without hesitation.

Section 09

Reporting Policy Violations

If you believe another user is violating this policy, or if you have received unauthorized scans from our platform, please report it immediately:

  • Email: abuse@penchecker.io
  • Include: Your contact information, description of the violation, evidence (logs, timestamps, URLs), and any relevant technical details

We investigate all reports and will respond within 48 hours. Reports are treated confidentially.

Responsible Disclosure

If you discover a security vulnerability in PenChecker itself, please report it responsibly to security@penchecker.io before public disclosure. We will acknowledge your report within 24 hours and work with you to resolve it. We do not take legal action against good-faith security researchers who follow responsible disclosure guidelines.

Section 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this AUP to reflect changes in law, platform capabilities, or our understanding of appropriate use. Material changes will be communicated to registered users at least 14 days before taking effect.

Questions about this policy? Contact legal@penchecker.io.
