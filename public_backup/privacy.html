Privacy Policy

Effective April 1, 2025

PenChecker Inc.
Plain English Summary

We collect data necessary to run the platform: your account info, scan results, payment data, and usage analytics. We do not sell your personal data to anyone. Scan results are yours. We keep security consent logs for legal compliance. You can request deletion of your personal data at any time.

Section 01

Overview

PenChecker Inc. ("we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our platform at penchecker.io and related services.

We are headquartered in San Francisco, California, United States, and operate in compliance with applicable US privacy laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Section 02

Data We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account: name, email address, password (hashed), company name, phone/WhatsApp number (optional), profile avatar (optional).

2.2 Scan & Technical Data

When you run scans: target URLs and domain names, scan results, vulnerability findings, HTTP request/response evidence, technology fingerprint data, security scores, and report exports.

2.3 Payment Data

Billing information is processed by Paystack. We store: subscription plan, billing cycle, payment status, and transaction references. We do not store full credit card numbers — these are handled exclusively by Paystack's PCI-compliant infrastructure.

2.4 Consent Logs (Special Category)

Every scan requires domain ownership confirmation. We permanently log: user ID, target URL, IP address at time of consent, user agent, and timestamp. These records are retained indefinitely for legal compliance and cannot be deleted by users.

2.5 Usage & Analytics Data

Pages visited, features used, scan frequency, session duration, browser type, operating system, IP address, and referring URLs — collected to improve the Service.

2.6 Communications

If you contact our support team, we retain those communications to provide support and improve our service.

Section 03

How We Use Your Data

We use collected data to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the PenChecker platform
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Send transactional emails (scan completion, billing, security alerts)
  • Send product updates and security tips (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized scanning
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features and performance
  • Provide customer support
What we never do

We never sell your personal data to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers. We do not use your scan results or vulnerability data to train AI models or share with competitors. Your security data is yours.

Section 04

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We share data only in these limited circumstances:

4.1 Service Providers

We use trusted third-party providers who process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements:

  • Local Secure Hosting — Database and authentication hosting
  • Paystack — Payment processing
  • Anthropic — AI assistant capabilities (message content only, no personal data)
  • Analytics provider — Aggregated, anonymized usage analytics

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government request. We will notify you of such requests unless legally prohibited from doing so.

4.3 Consent Logs for Legal Proceedings

Scan consent logs may be shared with law enforcement if we receive a lawful request related to alleged unauthorized scanning from your account.

4.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale, user data may be transferred to the acquiring entity, subject to the same privacy protections described in this policy.

Section 05

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide the Service and comply with our legal obligations. See the data table in Section 2 for specific retention periods.

When you delete your account, we begin the deletion process within 30 days. You may request an export of your data before deletion at privacy@penchecker.io.

Section 06

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2/1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Strict access policies in our database infrastructure
  • Multi-factor authentication available for all accounts
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing of our own platform
  • Access controls ensuring only authorized personnel can access production data
  • Service role keys and API credentials stored as environment variables, never in code

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by applicable law.

Section 07

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access (GDPR Art. 15) — Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you, delivered within 30 days
  • Correction (GDPR Art. 16) — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Erasure (GDPR Art. 17) — The "right to be forgotten." Request deletion of your personal data. We will erase your data within 30 days unless we are legally required to retain it
  • Portability (GDPR Art. 20) — Request your data in a structured, machine-readable JSON format for transfer to another service
  • Objection (GDPR Art. 21) — Object to processing of your personal data for direct marketing or legitimate interest purposes
  • Withdraw Consent — Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing
  • Opt-out — Opt out of marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email
  • Restriction (GDPR Art. 18) — Request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances, such as while disputing accuracy. To exercise any right, contact privacy@penchecker.io
    • that we limit the processing of your data in certain circumstances

California residents have additional rights under CCPA including the right to know, delete, and opt out of data sales (we do not sell data). EU/UK residents have rights under GDPR.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@penchecker.io. We will respond within 30 days.

Section 09

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, contact privacy@penchecker.io.

Section 10

International Data Transfers

PenChecker is based in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the US, your data will be transferred to and processed in the US. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as the legal mechanism for cross-border data transfers where required.
